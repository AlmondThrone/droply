PRIVACY

Droply Privacy Policy

This page explains the information Droply needs to operate its public Telegram bot and how supported files are processed.

WHAT DROPly PROCESSES

Information needed to provide the service.

💬

Telegram information

When you use the bot, Telegram provides information needed for Telegram interactions, such as your Telegram user identifier and message content. Droply uses necessary identifiers to operate the service, enforce usage limits and manage Pro access.

📁

Uploaded files

Supported files may be temporarily stored while a job is being processed. The public service is designed to use temporary processing files rather than a personal permanent file library.

🔗

Media URLs

When you submit a supported media URL, the service needs that URL to retrieve and process the requested media.

WHAT PUBLIC DROPly DOES NOT USE

No browser-cookie or PC access.

🍪

No browser cookies

The public Droply bot does not require browser cookies from your device.

🖥️

No PC access

The public bot does not access your PC files, screen or clipboard.

☁️

No personal cloud area

The public bot does not access a personal cloud drive as part of its public workflow.

PAYMENTS

Pro subscriptions.

Optional Pro subscriptions are processed through the configured payment provider, Razorpay. Payment details are handled by the payment provider rather than stored by Droply as card or bank credentials. Droply receives the payment/subscription information needed to verify and maintain Pro access.

YOUR CHOICES

Using or stopping the service.

🆓

Free use

You can use the Free tier without subscribing to Pro.

🛑

Stop using Droply

You can stop interacting with the bot at any time. Telegram also provides its own controls for managing your Telegram account and messages.

⭐

Pro cancellation

Pro subscriptions follow the subscription terms and cancellation flow provided by the payment provider. Access can remain available until the applicable paid period ends.

CONTACT

Questions about Droply?

For service questions or reports, use the Droply help bot from the main bot's help workflow. The public bot is available at @DroplyFileBot.
